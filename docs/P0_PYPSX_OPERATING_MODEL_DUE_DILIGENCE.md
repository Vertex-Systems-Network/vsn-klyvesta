# P0 — pyPSX Broker API Operating Model Due Diligence

Status: **ACTIVE / PARTIALLY VERIFIED**
Date: 2026-08-25
Canonical task: `P0-T1 — Confirm regulatory and pyPSX Broker API operating model`

## Purpose

This document converts public/official evidence and the Klyvesta acceptance gates into an explicit due-diligence matrix. It is not a substitute for legal advice, the pyPSX partner contract, or the underlying licensed broker's written confirmation.

No production real-money, personalized AI-advice, or Guarded Auto capability is unlocked by this document.

## Product modes under review

Klyvesta separates three customer modes because they may have materially different regulatory treatment:

1. **Manual** — customer originates and confirms each order.
2. **AI-assisted** — system provides analysis/recommendations; customer remains the final order decision-maker.
3. **Guarded Auto** — system can make portfolio/order decisions within a pre-agreed mandate and deterministic risk/compliance controls.

The product must not collapse these into one legal category.

## Public pyPSX evidence

Public pyPSX Broker API material currently supports the following high-level capability claims:

- account opening;
- KYC/AML flow;
- order routing;
- custody;
- T+1 settlement;
- reporting/statements;
- use cases including embedded investing, robo-advisor and model portfolios.

These are **marketing/public capability claims**, not implementation contracts.

Public material reviewed on 2026-08-25 is inconsistent on rollout state: fresher indexed material describes Broker API as live/onboarding partners, while older cached material described early access/sandbox as coming soon. Therefore Klyvesta treats sandbox availability, production readiness and exact endpoints as **UNVERIFIED** until the partner team provides current written technical documentation and credentials.

## Pakistan regulatory evidence — portfolio management / discretionary management

SECP's 14 November 2024 press release on the Securities Managers (Licensing and Operations) Regulations, 2024 states that:

- eligible securities brokers may provide portfolio-management services only after obtaining a Securities Manager licence from SECP;
- minimum net worth for the applicant is Rs 30 million;
- Broker Fiduciary Rating of BFR 2 or higher is required;
- adequate research capacity is required;
- customer funds and securities must be maintained with an independent custodian;
- the minimum investment a securities manager may accept from a client is Rs 5 million;
- the framework addresses the Investment Policy Statement and conflicts of interest.

Primary evidence: SECP press release, 14 November 2024, `Press-Release-Nov-14-Regulations-related-to-Securities-Managers.pdf`.

### Consequence for Klyvesta

The original product aspiration — "any retail user can deposit a small amount and AI will autonomously manage it" — **cannot be assumed legally available** under the current public evidence.

Guarded Auto must remain blocked until the exact structure is confirmed in writing. At least these alternatives must be evaluated:

- pyPSX / underlying broker has the required Securities Manager licence and contractually provides the regulated discretionary/portfolio-management service;
- Klyvesta partners with another appropriately licensed entity;
- Klyvesta obtains a relevant licence if legally available/appropriate;
- Guarded Auto is limited/restructured so it does not constitute regulated discretionary portfolio management, if counsel and regulator/partner explicitly confirm that treatment.

No implementation may attempt to evade the regulatory classification by renaming discretionary management as an "AI agent".

## Manual and AI-assisted treatment

Manual trading is architecturally separable from discretionary management because the customer remains the order originator/approver. However, production permission still depends on the licensed broker arrangement, IBTS/order-routing obligations, customer agreement/disclosures, custody/settlement and partner certification.

AI-assisted recommendations may create a separate investment-advisory/regulatory question. The fact that a recommendation is generated by software/AI does not remove the need to determine whether the activity is regulated advice and which licensed party is responsible.

Therefore `P3_AI_ASSISTED` remains blocked until partner/legal classification and disclosures are accepted.

## Customer legal-account model

Obtain unambiguous written answers to:

1. Which legal entity is the customer's securities broker?
2. Whose terms/customer agreement does the investor accept?
3. Who performs and owns KYC/AML approval?
4. Who creates/owns the NCCPL UIN relationship?
5. What CDC structure is used: broker sub-account, Investor Account, or another approved structure?
6. Who is the legal custodian of securities?
7. Where does customer cash reside before and after trade settlement?
8. Does any customer investment money ever pass through a Klyvesta corporate bank account? Expected answer for ordinary architecture: **no**.
9. Who is responsible for corporate actions/dividends/tax/settlement records?
10. What happens to customers if Klyvesta ceases operating?

## Architecture consequences already locked

Until P0 is complete:

- implement no real-money pyPSX adapter;
- do not expose broker credentials to any client or LLM;
- do not collect/pool customer investment funds in Klyvesta's normal corporate account;
- do not claim risk-free, zero-loss, or guaranteed profit;
- do not represent Guarded Auto as available to all small-ticket retail users;
- keep broker integration behind `BrokerAdapter`;
- ambiguous external financial side effects map to `UNKNOWN`, then query/reconcile rather than blind retry;
- paper/shadow architecture may progress only under its separate acceptance gate and repository-governance constraints.

## Decision record

The technical/product target remains an AI-native investment platform, but regulation is an architecture input rather than a post-launch compliance patch. The safest product sequencing is:

`Manual → AI-assisted (after permission) → Guarded Auto (only after discretionary-management permission/mandate controls)`.

This sequencing preserves the long-term product vision without fabricating legal authority that has not yet been obtained.
